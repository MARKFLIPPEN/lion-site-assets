Accessibility

Accessibility statement

How this site is built to be usable by everyone, what we have checked, and where it still falls short.

Updated 11 September 2026

The standard we work to

This site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. That is the target for every page, and the measure we test against.

What is in place

  • Text contrast is measured at 4.5 to 1 or better on every text and background pair, including text on the dark bands.
  • Links and buttons are at least 44 pixels tall on phones, so they can be hit with a thumb.
  • Keyboard focus is visible on every link and button, with a ring designed for the site rather than the browser default.
  • A skip link at the top of each page jumps past the navigation to the content.
  • Each page has one heading at the top, headings in order below it, and landmarks for navigation, main content and footer.
  • The charts on the home page carry written descriptions of what they show.
  • Motion is kept small, and if your device asks for reduced motion, the site drops the movement and keeps only short fades.
  • No video or audio plays automatically, and nothing flashes.
  • Text can be enlarged and the layout holds without sideways scrolling, down to a 360 pixel screen.

How we tested

Every page was checked in Chrome at phone, tablet and desktop widths from 360 to 1440 pixels, walked with the keyboard alone, and run through automated accessibility checks. Contrast was measured in the code, not judged by eye.

The site has not yet been tested with a screen reader. That is the next check, and this page will say so once it is done.

Known limits

  • The charts on the home page are images with a written summary. They are not data tables, so the individual figures cannot be read cell by cell.
  • Links to book a call or subscribe to the Brief open in a new tab, and do not yet announce that before they open.
  • One word in the home page headline changes every four seconds. It pauses under the mouse and stays still if your device asks for reduced motion, but it cannot yet be paused from the keyboard.
  • Booking runs on Calendly and the Brief runs on Kit. Those pages are theirs, and their accessibility is theirs to maintain.
  • Documents we send by email, such as assessment findings, are not covered by this statement. Ask and we will provide them in a format that works for you.

Tell us what is not working

If any part of this site is hard to use, email info@lionspecialty.com with the page and what went wrong. We will reply, fix what we can, and give you the content another way in the meantime.
