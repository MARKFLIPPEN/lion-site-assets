THE 8-POINT STRESS TEST

Eight questions, every client program, every year. The policy you send is read at the highlighted point; the rest are discussed from what you tell us.

Professional liability

Check that your policy describes the insurance work you perform

If you send your E&O policy, we examine these provisions where they appear. Underwriting and claims handling have to be read against your professional-services definition; the policy's name alone does not answer that question. We connect the activities you describe, and the allegations that reach carriers like yours, to the provisions that would respond, and mark the wording that needs negotiating.

  • WHAT WE CHECK IN YOUR POLICY
  • Professional-services definition against underwriting and claims handling
  • ECO/XPL provisions in the submitted policy, and the questions to take back to your reinsurance review
  • Claims-handling and bad-faith allegations against exclusions and carve-backs

Executive decisions

Explain the protection behind your board's own D&O

If you send your D&O policy, we examine these provisions where they appear. A regulated risk-bearing entity and its directors can have different interests the day a claim develops. A state examination, a reserving dispute after an acquisition, a shareholder or policyholder action are the kinds of events we discuss. We read Side A, severability, allocation, and the claim definition for the moment the institution's interests and yours diverge.

  • WHAT WE CHECK IN YOUR POLICY
  • Side A wording, and whether its limit is shared or dedicated
  • How conduct exclusions apply to different insureds, including severability and adjudication wording
  • Whether the claim definition includes the examination or proceeding you are concerned about, and which response costs it addresses

What to send

Send one issued D&O or E&O policy, declarations and endorsements included. If both coverages are written in one issued policy, send that complete policy. That is the whole preparation.

Within the 8-Point Stress Test, the policy is read through five questions. The remaining points are discussed from what you tell us about the program.

  • THE FIVE QUESTIONS
  • Who is insured?Which people, entities, and activities the wording includes.
  • When does coverage attach?The trigger, reporting requirements, and timing provisions.
  • Who controls the defense?Counsel selection, consent, settlement authority, and conditions.
  • What does the form take away?Exclusions, restrictions, and any exceptions that give coverage back.
  • How much is actually available?Limits, sublimits, retentions, and what erodes them.
