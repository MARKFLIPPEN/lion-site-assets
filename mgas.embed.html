The five questions

Who is insured?Which people, entities, and activities the wording includes.
When does coverage attach?The trigger, reporting requirements, and timing provisions.
Who controls the defense?Counsel selection, consent, settlement authority, and conditions.
What does the form take away?Exclusions, restrictions, and any exceptions that give coverage back.
How much is actually available?Limits, sublimits, retentions, and what erodes them.
