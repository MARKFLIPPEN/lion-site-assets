Privacy

Privacy policy

What this website collects, what it does not, and who else is involved when you contact us, book a call, or subscribe to the Brief.

Effective 11 September 2026

Who we are

LION Specialty LLC is a specialty insurance brokerage. We are a Wyoming limited liability company registered to do business in Colorado. Questions about this policy go to info@lionspecialty.com.

What this website collects

Very little. There are no forms on this site, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers.

  • Our hosting provider, Webflow, keeps standard server logs: the IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of each visit. Those logs exist to run and secure the service. We do not use them to identify you.
  • The hosting layer may set one strictly necessary cookie for security. It carries no personal information and we do not read it.
  • The site's fonts, stylesheet and script load from jsDelivr, a content delivery network. jsDelivr receives your IP address in order to serve those files, the same as any file you load from the web.

That is the full list. Nothing on this site is set up to follow you across other websites.

When you email us

Email to LION lands in our Microsoft 365 mailbox. We use what you send to reply, and if you go ahead with us, to do the work.

If you send a policy ahead of a free assessment, we read it for that assessment and keep it in our files. It is not sent to market, your current broker is not contacted, and it is not shared outside LION without your say-so.

When you book a call

Calls are booked through Calendly, on Calendly's website. Calendly collects your name, email address and the time you choose, under its own privacy policy, and passes the booking details to us so we can hold the slot and send an invitation.

When you subscribe to the Boardroom Brief

The Brief is sent through Kit. Signing up happens on Kit's website and gives Kit your email address, under Kit's own privacy policy. We use that address for one thing: sending the Brief. Every edition carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from the list.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • We do not run advertising on this site or share visitor data with advertisers.
  • We do not profile visitors or build audiences from site activity.
  • This site is written for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

How long we keep things

Server logs are kept by Webflow for as long as its own policy sets. Email and documents you send us are kept for as long as we need them to do the work and to meet the record-keeping duties of a licensed insurance brokerage.

Your choices

Email info@lionspecialty.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We will answer, and we will delete what we are not required to keep. To stop the Brief, use the unsubscribe link in any edition.

Changes to this policy

If the way this site works changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves.
